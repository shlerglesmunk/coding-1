Main objective: survive the chasers for at least 60 seconds
The timer may vary if you ever get anything that changes this timer.
after time runs out you would have to pick an enemy to put in the mix or sometimes a curse before doing it all over again.
every second you suffer though gives you 1 point, and that point is multiplied by the amount of chasers and curses you have.
Touching a chaser at any point kills you and you have to start from the very beginning.

You start with only being able to move but overtime you would get different abilities like dashing, or even teleporting.
dying obviously removes all of these abilities.

The chasers would each have a different method of catching you, and there can be multiple.
Maybe some wont even chase you, but they will be something obstructing your path.

Curses would mainly consist of something that debuffs you or buffs a chaser.
